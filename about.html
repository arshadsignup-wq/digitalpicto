Our Story

The Gaps Between
the Services.

The pattern showed up again and again. A business would hire one company for the website, another for the ads, a freelancer for SEO, and somebody's cousin for the spreadsheet that held it all together. Every piece worked. The business still was not growing.

You could usually find the reason in a single afternoon. Leads arriving on a Friday and getting called on Monday. A beautiful site with no clear next step on it. An owner doing three hours of copying and pasting every week because no one had ever been asked to automate it.

Nobody was responsible for those gaps, because each gap sat between two suppliers. That is the problem Digital Picto was built to take ownership of: the marketing, the technology and the day-to-day operations treated as one system rather than four invoices.

It is still how we work. We would rather fix the handoff that is losing you work than sell you a fifth service that has to be handed off again.

The services aren't the problem.
The gaps between them are.

The People Behind Digital Picto

Two founders,
one accountable team.

Two people started this, and the same two people are still the ones in your account. That is the whole arrangement: no handover to a junior team once the contract is signed.

Arshad Hossain

Co-founder · Meta Ads & SEO

Arshad came to this work from the side of it that nobody advertises. He spent his early years in paid social learning that most accounts do not fail because the ads are bad. They fail because nobody has looked closely enough at what happens after the click.

That habit of looking closely is what he brought with him. He runs the Meta Ads and search work here, and his instinct on a new account is always the same: find the step where the money is quietly leaving, and fix that before touching anything creative.

He has also kept an unusual habit for this industry. Everything he has worked on, he writes up in full on his own site, dashboards included, not a tidied summary of them. If you want to see how he thinks before you talk to him, that is the place to look.

Fahim Kabir

Co-founder · Google Ads & delivery

Fahim works at the end of the market where the auction is expensive and the rules are strict: insurance, finance, healthcare. It is unglamorous territory, and it taught him something useful early on: in a regulated category, knowing exactly what you are allowed to say is not a restriction. It is the advantage.

He holds Google's own certifications in Search and Analytics, though what clients tend to notice is quieter than that. He is the person who reads the policy before writing the ad, and who builds the page before the campaign that points at it.

He is also the project manager on every virtual assistant engagement we run. One person who knows the brief, did the onboarding and can tell you on a Friday what moved that week. Not a shared inbox, and not a queue.
