Our Process

How We Set Up Your
GoHighLevel Account

From initial audit to full handover, here is exactly how we get your GoHighLevel platform configured and running.

01

GHL Audit & Planning

We start by reviewing your current setup or business needs and creating a custom GHL roadmap. If you already have an account, we audit what is working and what is not. If you are starting fresh, we map out the exact features, workflows, and integrations you need based on your goals. This planning phase ensures every decision we make during setup is intentional and aligned with how your business actually operates.

02

Account Configuration

We set up your GoHighLevel account from the ground up with proper domains, integrations, and settings. This includes connecting your custom domain, configuring email and SMS sending, setting up phone numbers, establishing user roles and permissions, and integrating with your existing tools. Everything is configured correctly the first time so you do not run into deliverability issues or broken connections down the road.

03

Automation & Workflow Build

This is where the real power of GoHighLevel comes to life. We design and build the automations, workflows, and funnels that drive your business forward. Lead nurturing sequences, appointment reminders, follow-up campaigns, review requests, and pipeline stage triggers are all configured and tested thoroughly. Every automation is built with clear logic and proper conditions so it runs reliably without constant supervision.

04

Migration & Data Import

If you are coming from another platform, we move your contacts, conversations, and data into GoHighLevel cleanly and accurately. We handle the export from your old system, map fields correctly, clean up duplicates, and verify that everything lands in the right place. Your historical data stays intact so you do not lose years of customer information in the transition.

05

Training & Handover

Once everything is built and tested, we train your team on how to use GoHighLevel effectively. We walk through every workflow, pipeline, and dashboard so your team understands how the system works and how to manage it day to day. We provide documentation and recordings for reference, and ongoing support is available if you need help down the line. You walk away confident in using the platform, not confused by it.
