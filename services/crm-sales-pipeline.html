CRM & Sales
Pipeline Management

Most SMEs lose leads every single day, not because they lack interest from potential customers, but because they have no system to track and manage those leads. Enquiries come in through the website, social media, phone calls, and referrals, and they end up scattered across inboxes and forgotten spreadsheets. A properly set up CRM and sales pipeline changes that completely. It gives you full visibility into every lead, where they are in the buying journey, and exactly what needs to happen next to move them closer to a sale. We do not just set up software and hand you a login. We build a complete lead management system tailored to how your business actually sells, so your team always knows who to call, when to follow up, and which deals are most likely to close.

Our Process

How We Build Your
Sales Pipeline

Five focused steps to turn scattered leads into a structured, revenue-generating pipeline.

01

Pipeline Assessment

We start by understanding your current sales process inside and out. How do leads come in? What happens after first contact? Where do deals stall or fall through the cracks? We map your entire sales journey, identify the gaps, and pinpoint exactly where a structured pipeline and CRM setup will have the biggest impact on your revenue.

02

CRM Selection & Setup

Based on your business size, sales process, and budget, we recommend and configure the right CRM platform for your needs. We set up user accounts, customize the interface, and configure all the core settings so the tool feels intuitive for your team from day one. Everything is tailored to your workflow, not the other way around.

03

Pipeline Architecture

This is where the system takes shape. We design your pipeline stages, build automation rules for lead routing and task assignments, set up lead scoring criteria, and configure the triggers that keep deals moving forward. Every rule and workflow is mapped to your actual sales process so the system runs efficiently and nothing gets overlooked.

04

Data Migration & Training

We import your existing contacts, clean up duplicates, set up integrations with your website, email, and ad platforms, and make sure data flows smoothly into the new system. Then we train your team on how to use the CRM effectively. We walk through every feature they need, answer questions, and make sure everyone is confident before we step back.

05

Optimization & Scaling

Once your pipeline is live, we monitor how it performs and look for opportunities to improve. Are leads getting stuck at a certain stage? Is the follow-up timing right? Are your close rates going up? We refine automations, adjust scoring models, and tweak the pipeline structure based on real performance data so your system keeps getting better and scales with your business.

Lead scoring assigns a numerical value to each lead based on their actions and profile.
