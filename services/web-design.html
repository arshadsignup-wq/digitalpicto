Our Process

How We Build Your Website

Five clear steps from first conversation to a website that actually grows your business.

01

Discovery and Strategy

We start by getting to know your business inside and out. What are your goals? Who are your customers? What do your competitors do well, and where do they fall short? This research shapes every design and development decision that follows, so we never skip it.

02

Wireframing and Design

Before we write a single line of code, we map out the user experience. Wireframes show you exactly how each page will flow, where key elements will sit, and how visitors will navigate your site. Then we layer on the visual design, making sure it feels like your brand and speaks to your audience.

03

Development and Integration

This is where the design comes to life. Our developers build your website with clean, efficient code and integrate your CMS, payment gateways, booking systems, analytics tools, and any other third-party platforms your business relies on. Everything is built to work together seamlessly.

04

Testing and Quality Assurance

We test your site on every major browser and device. We check load speeds, fix accessibility issues, run through every form and button, and stress-test the whole experience. Nothing goes live until we are confident it works perfectly for every visitor.

05

Launch and Optimization

Going live is just the beginning. We handle the launch carefully, monitor performance closely in the first few weeks, and use real visitor data to make smart improvements. Your website keeps getting better over time, not worse.
