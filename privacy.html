Privacy Policy

Last updated 14 November 2026

Who we are

Digital Picto ("we", "us") operates the website at digitalpicto.online. For any question about this policy or your data, contact Digitalpicto832@gmail.com.

Digital Picto is a digital marketing and automation practice operating online, founded by Arshad Hossain and Fahim Kabir. We serve clients internationally and do not operate a public walk-in office; all enquiries are handled by email.

What we collect

We collect personal data only when you choose to send it to us.

  • Contact form. Your name, email address, business type, indicative budget and the content of your message.
  • Email. Anything you include when you write to us directly.
  • Server logs. Our hosting provider records standard request data, including IP address, browser type and the pages requested.

We do not ask for payment card details through this website, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16.

Why we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry and discuss whether we can help.
  • To provide services you engage us for.
  • To keep records of our business correspondence.

The lawful basis is your consent when you submit the form, and our legitimate interest in responding to business enquiries.

Who processes it

We use a small number of third parties. Each receives only what it needs.

  • Formspree: processes contact form submissions and forwards them to us by email. Their privacy terms apply to that processing.
  • Google Fonts: typefaces load from Google's servers, which means Google receives your IP address when a page loads.
  • Our hosting provider: stores and serves this website, and keeps standard server access logs as described above.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not share it for advertising.

Cookies and tracking

This website sets no cookies of its own and runs no advertising or analytics trackers. If that changes, we will update this policy and ask for consent where the law requires it.

How long we keep it

Enquiry correspondence is kept for as long as needed to respond and for a reasonable period afterwards for business records. You may ask us to delete it sooner.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or receive a copy of your personal data, and to object to or restrict how we use it. To exercise any of these, email Digitalpicto832@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Security

This site is served over HTTPS. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.

Governing law

Digital Picto serves clients internationally. Where local data protection law gives you stronger rights than this policy describes, for example the GDPR in the UK and EU or the CCPA in California, those rights apply and we will honour them. Write to us and we will respond within 30 days.
