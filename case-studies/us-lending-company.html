45 leads across two lending accounts

Two Facebook Ads lead generation campaigns for a US lending company produced 45 leads at $7.98 and $11.21 cost per lead, with a 100/100 Meta Opportunity Score on both accounts.

The problem

A US lending company needed qualified leads for two different products, mortgage lending and lease buyout, through Facebook Ads.

What we did

  • Built two separate lead generation campaigns, one per product
  • Structured targeting around high-intent financial searchers
  • Facebook Pixel and Conversions API for accurate lead tracking
  • Bids and audiences optimised daily

How it ran

Both campaigns ran the same method with the same operator against the same market, which made the gap between them readable rather than noise.

The campaigns remain active and continue to generate leads.

What the numbers actually show

A $3.23 gap between two products run identically is the useful part. Lease buyout is a narrower intent than general lending, so the addressable audience is smaller and the auction thinner. That cost difference is a property of the offer, not the execution. Both accounts hit 100/100 on Meta's Opportunity Score, which measures setup completeness, not profitability.

What to know about these figures

This client is anonymous and spend is not disclosed, so cost per lead here cannot be cross-checked the way it can on the named accounts.

Full reporting for this campaign, including the raw dashboards, is published on Arshad Hossain’s portfolio.
