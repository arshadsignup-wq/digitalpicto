The problem

Tombizmerch sells World Cup merchandise solutions to embroidery shops, clothing brands and retail owners. A World Cup is a fixed window: buyers needed a merch-ready storefront before the tournament started, so there was no time for a careful three-week test phase.

What we did

  • Three separate lead generation campaigns, one per market segment
  • Segment one: embroidery businesses already set up to decorate blanks
  • Segment two: small clothing brands looking for a seasonal line
  • Segment three: retail owners in custom merchandise
  • Consolidated, algorithm-first structure across all three

How it ran

With no time to iterate toward a winner, three clean campaigns ran simultaneously against different slices of the market, and the market itself decided which one answered.

All three ran for seven days and returned 64 qualified B2B leads at $12.23 average, with three moving into active sales conversations.

What the numbers actually show

When the calendar is the constraint, you stop trying to out-think the targeting and run several clean campaigns at once against different segments, then read which one the market answers. Speed came from structure, not from cleverness.
