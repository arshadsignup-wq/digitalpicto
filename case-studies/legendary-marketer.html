The problem

Legendary Marketer sells affiliate marketing education, with the 15-Day Business Builder Challenge as its lead product and a $1,497 course behind it. The campaign had to generate signups efficiently enough for that back end to pay out.

What we did

  • Single Themed Ad Group (STAG) structure
  • Keywords segmented for precise thematic targeting
  • Automated bidding set to maximise clicks within budget
  • Broad, phrase and exact match used deliberately to control how closely keywords matched search terms

How it ran

Keywords were chosen on search intent rather than volume, which is what produced both the high click-through rate and the conversion rate behind it.

An already high-performing landing page funnel carried the traffic, and over one hundred signups followed, with a number going on to buy the $1,497 course.

What the numbers actually show

A 5.99% CTR at $0.36 is the result of matching intent before optimising bids. The funnel mattered as much as the campaign: a strong landing page was doing half the work the ads are usually credited with.

  • Exceeded: ROI target on back-end course sales
