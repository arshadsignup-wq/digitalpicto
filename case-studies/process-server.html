From campaign error to 142 leads

A stalled Google Ads account for a US process server was rebuilt from a campaign error and not-spending state into 142 leads, with ROI up 31% and cost per click down 17%.

The problem

The account had three problems at once: no conversions, an active campaign error, and budget that was not spending at all. Nothing could be judged until the account was actually running.

What we did

  • Full campaign restructure
  • Fixed the campaign error blocking delivery
  • Conversion tracking set up with Google Tag Manager and GA4
  • Ad copy optimisation
  • Budget and bidding optimisation
  • Negative keyword filtering

How it ran

The error and the delivery problem came first. An account that is not spending cannot be optimised, and no amount of keyword work would have surfaced that.

Once delivery was restored and tracking was in place, $967.78 of spend produced 2,000 clicks, 18,000 interactions and 142 leads, with ROI up 31% and cost per click down 17%.

What the numbers actually show

Not every paid account needs a new strategy. This one needed somebody to look at why it was not spending, which is the least glamorous and highest-return hour in paid search.
