From $86 to $3.91 a lead

The problem

A US hair clinic was running Facebook Ads on a $50/day budget and paying $86 for every lead. At that price the channel could not pay for itself, and the account had been left running rather than rebuilt.

What we did

  • Tested 35 Facebook Ads campaigns across audiences, creatives and placements
  • Installed Facebook Pixel and Conversions API so attribution was clean before optimising
  • Drove cost per lead down in deliberate stages rather than one broad change
  • Pushed budget into winning ad sets only once they had proven out

How it ran

The first round established the baseline at $86 per lead. Audience targeting was reworked, underperforming creative was cut, and placements were narrowed. That second phase brought cost per lead to roughly $14.

The final step was almost entirely creative. Testing combinations against the narrowed audience settled the active campaign at $3.91 per lead with 27 website leads and a 94/100 Meta Opportunity Score.

What the numbers actually show

The drop happened in two distinct steps, not gradually: $86 to about $14, then $14 to $3.91. The first came from fixing measurement and narrowing the audience; the second came from creative. The account had two separate problems, and solving only one would have stalled the account at $14.
