Automating a solar customer journey

An automation build for a solar business covering the customer journey from first enquiry through qualification, appointment booking and follow-up, with a recorded walkthrough of the live system.

The problem

Solar is a long, consultative sale with a high ticket and a slow decision. Enquiries arrive faster than a small team can qualify them by hand, and the ones that are not contacted quickly are usually gone.

What we did

  • Mapped the real customer journey before automating any part of it
  • Automated lead qualification so the team only speaks to viable prospects
  • Appointment booking with confirmations and reminders
  • Follow-up sequences for prospects not yet ready to commit
  • Internal notifications and CRM updates without manual data entry

How it ran

The build connects the stages that were previously joined by somebody remembering: enquiry, qualification, booking, reminder, follow-up.

The recorded walkthrough shows the live workflow end to end.

What the numbers actually show

In a considered purchase, speed of first response matters more than polish of first response. Automating qualification and booking buys back the hours that decide whether a prospect is still in the market when you reach them.
