The problem

A roofing company needed consistent lead flow from Facebook Ads to keep its pipeline full, in one of the most expensive verticals on the platform.

What we did

  • Built and managed a $12,900 Facebook Ads lead generation campaign
  • Targeted homeowners inside the service area through Meta segmentation
  • Optimised creative and copy specifically for high-ticket home services
  • Daily monitoring and bid optimisation

How it ran

The campaign reached 36,218 people and served 75,232 impressions, producing 70 leads at $66.60 each from $4,661.96 of the budget.

What the numbers actually show

$66.60 per lead sounds high until you place it. Published 2026 benchmarks put roofing above $115, so this is roughly 40% under category. Contract value changes the economics too: a job worth several thousand dollars absorbs a $66 lead in a way a $60 product never could.

What to know about these figures

This campaign is still running. It has spent $4,661.96 of a $12,900 budget, so these figures are a snapshot and cost per lead may move in either direction as the budget completes.

Full reporting for this campaign, including the raw dashboards, is published on Arshad Hossain’s portfolio.
