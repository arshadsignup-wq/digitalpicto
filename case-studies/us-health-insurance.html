9.90% conversion rate in a regulated market

A US health insurance lead generation campaign achieved a 9.90% conversion rate and 8.72% click-through rate at $23.50 cost per conversion, in one of the most competitive and regulated categories on Google Ads.

The problem

US health insurance is a trillion-dollar category, which makes it both highly competitive and heavily regulated. Ad copy has to satisfy compliance rules while still speaking to genuine buyer intent.

What we did

  • Maximum Conversions bidding to optimise for conversions rather than clicks
  • Single Themed Ad Groups (STAG) for precise thematic relevance
  • A heavily optimised landing page to raise page quality score and win more impressions
  • Broad, phrase and exact match keywords used in combination

How it ran

The landing page and site were built first, before any keyword work, because page quality score directly affects how often ads serve in this category.

Intensive keyword research and split testing on ad copy followed, producing metrics above the category average and genuinely qualified leads.

What the numbers actually show

In a regulated category, compliance knowledge is a performance advantage, not an overhead. Understanding what can and cannot be said lets you write copy that resonates with policyholders without triggering disapprovals that stall a campaign.
