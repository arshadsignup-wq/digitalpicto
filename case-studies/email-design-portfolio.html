The problem

Email is the channel most businesses own outright and the one most often left to a default template. Each sector has its own conventions: an e-commerce launch, a bookkeeping newsletter and an event invitation are not the same document with different colours.

What we did

  • Campaign emails for e-commerce launches, promotions and seasonal pushes
  • Welcome and onboarding sequences
  • Newsletters for professional services and publishers
  • Event invitations, reminders and follow-ups
  • Product and catalogue emails for retail and fashion
  • Built in Klaviyo, Mailchimp and hand-coded responsive HTML

How it ran

The library spans fashion and luxury clothing, real estate, baby products, skincare, books, food, pet care, CBD and herbal, travel, restaurants, spiritual, education consultancy, bookkeeping, event management and more.

Each template is built responsive and tested in client inboxes rather than only in a design tool, because email clients break layouts that browsers render correctly.

What the numbers actually show

Volume across sectors is itself the useful asset. Having built the same mechanism for 28 different audiences means the format decisions on a new brief start from evidence rather than from a blank canvas.

What to know about these figures

This is a design portfolio. Open and click rates belong to the clients who sent these campaigns and are not published here.
