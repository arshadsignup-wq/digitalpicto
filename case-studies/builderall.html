The problem

Builderall is a digital marketing platform with 40+ tools. The campaign needed to maximise lead generation within a fixed budget over a defined period, in a category where cost per click is routinely bid up by competitors.

What we did

  • Single Themed Ad Group (STAG) structure across Search
  • Keywords segmented for precise thematic targeting
  • Automated bidding with maximum CPC
  • Targeted Spanish-speaking audiences across North and South America, an underserved segment

How it ran

A Spanish-language funnel was built to carry the traffic, with conversions tracked inside Builderall itself.

Ad copy and landing pages were optimised to lead with value, including a weekly newsletter of internet marketing tips, rather than pushing straight to signup.

The structure was adapted ahead of a Google Ads update so performance held through the change.

What the numbers actually show

Lower cost per click than both competitors and the category average came from language, not from bidding. The Spanish-speaking segment was underserved, so the auction was thinner and the same budget bought considerably more.

  • Positive ROI: Maintained across the full 4-month run
  • Top 100: Client ranked among all Builderall affiliates
