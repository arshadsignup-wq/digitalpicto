The problem

Most businesses do not have a lead problem so much as a follow-up problem. Leads arrive through a form or an ad and then sit in an inbox until they go cold, because nothing automatically routes, reminds or escalates them.

What we did

  • High-converting sales funnels and landing pages
  • Lead capture forms and qualification surveys
  • Calendar booking with automatic confirmation
  • CRM pipeline and opportunity stages that match the real sales process
  • Automated email and SMS follow-up sequences
  • Appointment reminders and no-show nurture
  • Internal team notifications so a hot lead reaches a person
  • Conversion tracking so the system can be judged
  • Reputation and review requests after delivery

How it ran

Each build starts from the client's actual sales process rather than a template, because a pipeline that does not match how the team sells gets abandoned within a month.

The two walkthroughs below record live systems: the first covers pipeline, automation and follow-up logic, the second covers the funnel, booking flow and internal notifications.

What the numbers actually show

The value is not in any single automation but in the handoffs between them. A lead captured, qualified, booked, reminded and followed up without anyone remembering to do it is the difference between a channel that compounds and one that leaks.
