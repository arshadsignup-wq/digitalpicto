A digital build for healthcare

A healthcare-focused digital project combining a professional web presence with the supporting business processes behind it, built for Optimoflow360.

The problem

Healthcare buyers apply a higher trust threshold than most sectors. A site has to look credible to a clinical audience and still function as a working front end for the processes behind it.

What we did

  • Professional web presence built for a clinical audience
  • Clear structure for services and capabilities
  • Supporting business processes connected to the front end
  • Technical foundation suitable for a regulated sector

How it ran

The build pairs the public site with the operational processes behind it, so the website is a working part of the business rather than a brochure attached to it.

The project is live and publicly visible at optimoflow360.com.

What the numbers actually show

In healthcare, credibility is the conversion mechanism. Buyers are assessing whether an organisation looks like one that can be trusted with clinical work long before they assess the offer itself.
