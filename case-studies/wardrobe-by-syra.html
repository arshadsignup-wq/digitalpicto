Meta Ads · Bangladesh

Four campaigns, four objectives, one audience

Four Meta campaigns for a Bangladeshi fashion brand across two Eid seasons: 12,813 Messenger conversations at $0.04, 121 website purchases at $0.71, and an organic Reel that reached 333,110 views.

The problem

A Bangladeshi fashion brand needed to convert two Eid seasons into orders. The useful question was not which campaign worked, but how four different objectives would behave against the same audience and market.

What we did

  • Two click-to-Messenger campaigns aimed at conversation volume
  • One website conversion campaign aimed at purchases
  • One organic creative push with no paid support
  • Same audience and market across all four so the objectives could be compared

How it ran

The Eid ul Adha messaging campaign produced 12,813 conversations at $0.04 on $576.51 of spend, reaching 514,208 people.

A second message test averaged $0.03 per conversation, with the best ad set reaching $0.02 on $135.49 and 225,566 reach.

The product conversion campaign returned 121 website purchases at $0.71 average on $85.69.

The seasonal Reel ran organically with no spend at all and reached 333,110 views, 96% of them non-followers, which the client reported as 40,000 Tk of additional outlet sales.

What the numbers actually show

The same audience answers completely differently depending on what you ask it for. Conversations cost cents; purchases cost under a dollar; the organic Reel cost nothing and outperformed both on reach. Choosing the objective was the campaign decision that mattered most.
